## Program at a glance

The M.Sc. Computer Science (Major AI) is 120 ECTS over 4 semesters, 30 ECTS each, taught onsite in English at VGU's Faculty of Engineering. Source: MCS Module Handbook v1.0 (22 Sep 2026, still marked Draft, 92 pages).

| Study abroad | None required; all ECTS delivered at VGU |

## Semester 1 study packs

Each pack has 10 lectures with exercises and a real-world project.

- Core 1 — Mathematics of Computing: Lectures, Exercises, Project — project: product recommender
- Core 2 — Advanced Algorithms: Lectures, Exercises, Project — project: delivery routing on a real city map
- Core 3 — Deep Learning: Lectures, Exercises, Project — project: rice leaf disease detector on a phone
- Spec 1 — Natural Language Processing: Lectures, Exercises, Project — project: bilingual student-support assistant (RAG)
- Doc — project: motorbike traffic monitoring

## Assessment

Most 6-ECTS modules are graded 20% assignments, 30% group project and 50% closed-book exam. Every graded piece needs an AI Disclosure Statement (Article 8 of the Specific Examination Regulation); attendance is expected but never graded.

**Standard pattern**

- Assignments (20%): four individual tasks, about 8–10 hours each.
- Group project (30%): teams of 3–4, a software artefact plus a 10–15 page report, then a 15-minute team presentation with individual questioning.
- Final exam (50%): written, 90–120 minutes, closed book, no materials.

| Module | Different from the standard |
| --- | --- |
| Spec 2 Foundation Models | Assignments 30%, group project 20% |
| Spec 4 Robotics | 50% individual lab exam (60–90 min) on the faculty robot instead of a written exam |
| E6 AI Law, Ethics and Policy | Assignments are 1,500–2,000 word analyses; project is a 15–20 page compliance assessment; 50% is a 48-hour open-book take-home case (2,500–3,000 words) |
| Advanced Seminar | 50% critical review (8–10 pages) + peer review; 50% talk of 20–25 min + 5–10 min discussion |
| IKQ Scientific Writing | 60% LaTeX writing portfolio (four pieces); 40% proposal presentation of 20–25 min |
| Master Thesis | 80% thesis report (two examiners); 20% colloquium |

**Master Thesis rules**

- Start condition: at least 60 ECTS earned and a topic approved by a supervisor, then registered with the MCS office.
- Processing time: 6 months. The topic can be returned once, only in the first month.
- Colloquium: a 25–35 minute talk, 45–60 minutes in total, held within 4 weeks of submission, usually public.
- Workload: 900 AH (about 30 contact hours of supervision, 870 independent).

## Program learning outcomes

The program defines 10 outcomes (ILOs), and every one is graded in at least one module all students take. The column on the right lists where each is graded ("A" in the curriculum map).

| ILO | Graduates can… | Graded in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Knowledge | Explain math, algorithms, learning theory, HPC and data foundations | Core 1, Core 2 |
| 2 Knowledge | Explain what modern AI methods can and cannot do | Core 3, Spec 2, Spec 4 |
| 3 Application | Design, build and evaluate ML/AI systems | Core 3, Spec 1, Spec 3 |
| 4 Application | Run data and compute infrastructure for AI at scale | Core 4, Core 5, E4 |
| 5 Research | Plan and carry out independent research | Thesis |
| 6 Research | Validate results statistically and state limitations | Spec 3, Thesis |
| 7 Communication | Present technical work in English, written and oral | Advanced Seminar, IKQ, Thesis |
| 8 Cooperation | Deliver team projects with documentation, version control, reproducibility | Core 5 |
| 9 Professionalism | Judge legal, ethical and societal impact; use generative AI tools by the rules | E6, Thesis |
| 10 Self-conception | Judge own competence and plan further learning | Thesis |
